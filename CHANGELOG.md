# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Copycat skins now show on vanilla maps

## 1.0.0

- Plants can be placed on copycats wearing a skin that supports them
- Support for Copycats+ blocks and slabs, including double slabs
- Plants sit half a block lower on bottom slabs, with the hitbox moved and targeting from the side
- Plants break when the skin below stops supporting them
- Tall plants, berry bushes and other plants without collision can be lowered
- Copycat skins show on Xaero's Minimap and World Map
- Config for saplings, crops, a plant blacklist, bottom slab lowering and map support
